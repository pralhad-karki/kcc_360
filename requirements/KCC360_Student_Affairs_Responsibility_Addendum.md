## Shared controls

Student Affairs' own department plan and its subordinate programme/club plans retain owner→responsible-unit review→Finance Administrator→Principal final approval, including zero-cost plans and material changes. Club mentor review precedes the Student Affairs unit recommendation where applicable. Current accountable units and independently eligible reviewers must be explicitly assigned; no self-approval or invented named grant.

This is the plan approval route, not a universal requirement to send every individual placement application, employer decision, mentor appointment or student evaluation through Finance/Principal. Each retains its established case-specific authority. Academic workshops/seminars stay with Academic Department and assigned subject teacher/mentor for roster-based assignment. Academic/research proposals retain their separate routes. Student Affairs coordination does not replace them.

All services should reuse shared planning, tasks, evidence, notifications, monitoring, evaluations, reports and audit history, with programme-specific responsibility and privacy scope. Reuse does not make the underlying recruitment/assignment/placement routes identical. Stage264 addresses one club-plan authorization defect; broader departmental monitoring and placement delivery remain the next workstream, not completed functionality.

## Annual strategic targets and subordinate club plans — confirmed clarification

The user confirmed this planning hierarchy on 26 October 2026:

Strategic plan → Principal-issued annual targets for each department → departmental annual plan → subordinate club/programme plans → named activities → teams/tasks → evidence and evaluation.

For example, SDSN may propose three major and twelve minor events for a particular year. These numbers are an example, not an automatically provisioned quota for every club. Club targets contribute to the Student Affairs plan. Every department prepares its annual plan against its own issued targets. Existing seven-pillar/twenty-five-goal architecture must remain unchanged; annual target records must refer to stable strategic/KPI identifiers and versions rather than create a replacement KPI catalogue.

Once a club plan receives final approval through responsible-unit review → Finance Administrator → Principal, its activity names and approved dates appear in the Student Affairs dashboard. Pending amendments retain the previous approved schedule; finally approved successors replace it without duplicating activities or losing historical approvals. Approval establishes planned work, not verified KPI achievement or execution authority. Department/institution totals must distinguish allocations, planned contributions and verified results, and avoid counting the same contribution twice.

Principal issuance of departmental targets is distinct from authorship/final approval of an operational plan. This clarification grants no demo persona a live Principal role and does not bypass the existing principal-originated-plan independence policy. Issued target changes and locked plan changes require versioned, audited authority. Major/minor is an explicit classification, not inferred from title, budget or priority.

Stage 267 delivers only the protected approved-club-calendar projection. Versioned Principal target issuance, stable department/club KPI allocation, explicit major/minor targets, achievement aggregation and shared periodic monitoring/evaluation remain incremental implementation gaps. Current text KPI/target fields are planning declarations, not stable strategic links. Institutional identity/reviewer configuration remains required before live approvals.
