## Exception checks

- **Duplicate / unauthorized handoff:** Repeat the same authorized handoff, and inspect controls as student, mentor or unrelated staff. Expected: One project only; only the coordinator may perform the current handoff.
- **Calendar and duplicate logbook:** Try early project start, future/out-of-placement work date or duplicate entry for the same current date. Expected: Invalid action rejected without overwriting valid work; do not bypass the date gate.
- **Completion with unfinished work:** Inspect final submission/completion while tasks, logbooks or mentor follow-ups remain unresolved. Expected: Completion prerequisites stay enforced. Returned or stale task assessments are not verified completion.
- **Final evidence or mentor changes:** In a separate controlled branch, amend evidence or transfer mentor using existing approvals after a final review. Expected: Earlier decisions remain historical; current review/authorization is rechecked and fresh review is required where invalidated.
- **Private scope:** Check the project/logbook/final packet as a peer student, former mentor or unrelated supporting role. Expected: Private records/files remain unavailable outside current authorized scope; general project visibility is not private internship access.
- **Post-handoff source restrictions:** Inspect placement/readiness editing after handoff; use a separate letter-revocation branch if authorized. Expected: Direct source edits are locked. Revocation restricts dependent execution and raises source-change follow-up without deleting logs.
- **Recognition boundaries:** After execution/final approval, inspect evaluations, certificates, portfolio and MIS/LMS state. Expected: No automatic official grade/credit, certificate, public portfolio or website publication. Individual evaluation and separate authorized recognition remain required.
