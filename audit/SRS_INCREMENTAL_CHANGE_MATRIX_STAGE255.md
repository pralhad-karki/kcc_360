## Explicit conflicts and adoption rules

- AP-001 supersedes Department Head final academic-plan approval and Student Affairs final club-plan approval. Existing approved versions retain their actual historical approvers; no Principal or Finance review may be invented. Plan approval remains separate from signing, payment, settlement, mentor acceptance, evaluation, certificates and publication.
- WF-014 course routing remains subject-faculty first review with Coordinator fallback. Research now adds Academic Department screening/endorsement before Research Department review and mentor coordination. Retain one proposal ID and versioned forwarding history; do not create duplicate proposals. Existing prototype shared Coordinator triage is not compliant production routing.
- M08 personnel confirmation by Principal is distinct from M30 plan review. Principal plan approval alone cannot appoint a committee or activate access.
- PM-001–005 add assigned class rosters and approved non-class entry, session attendance, role-specific recognition, capacity/waitlists and external records without accounts. Club membership/application/registration alone grants neither task access nor verified achievements.
- Annex M CR-001–012 requires locked principal-approved baselines, separate versioned requests, mandatory zero-cost/date-only Finance review, atomic successor approval, rebase on concurrent changes, late-date history and idempotent dependent propagation. Current draft-version controls are partial foundations, not Annex M compliance.
- Reports use organizer review, then Principal and Finance completion/settlement. This is not the pre-execution Finance Administrator → Principal plan route. Eligible individual learning recognition may precede settlement.
- Structural file checks are implemented; malware scanning is not. The SRS wording cannot be treated as delivered scanning.
- All new AT-Mxx, AT-Xxx, AT-PM01 and AT-CRxx scenarios remain NOT RUN unless a later evidence record explicitly closes a bounded scenario. Existing tests do not certify the full new catalogue.

## Ordered implementation

1. Shared exact-version plan approval/authority foundation and historical reconciliation, then academic and club route/controller/UI/storage integration.
2. Shared approved-baseline change requests and lock enforcement across UI/API/import; concurrent rebase and dependent propagation.
3. Atomic membership/participant capacities, explicit roster/entry states and role-specific attendance/recognition.
4. Committee appointment/letter/scoped activation and succession; faculty scoped responsibilities and research forwarding.
5. Structured reflections and human fallback; joint event/partnership/external achievement/meeting workflows using shared records.

## Policy and external inputs

No question blocks the matrix or independent work. Live Principal-originated plan handling requires a recorded independent-authority rule; no self-approval exception is assumed. Named approvers/signatories, committee term/selection method, role templates, rubric weights, ethics/IP/retention and external academic-credit recognition authority remain configuration/policy inputs. Official MIS API/SSO and real email setup remain external dependencies; no endpoints/protocols are invented.
