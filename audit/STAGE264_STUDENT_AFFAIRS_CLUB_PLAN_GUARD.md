## Implemented

- `prototype/club-plan-governance.js` extends existing club controls; shared browser/engine source, not a second planning module. Student Affairs' former Approve annual plan control becomes Review and endorse plan. Independent owner-demo Student Affairs review requires a valid current plan, exact version, reason and explicit confirmation. It records UNIT_ENDORSED with an immutable scoped review record stating finalApproval:false and executionAuthorized:false. It creates no finance/Principal decision, approved snapshot or role grant.
- Repeated endorsement is a no-op. Student Affairs may withdraw its pending endorsement and return the plan to draft with a reason; prior recommendation and withdrawal remain in append-only history. A revised submission passes the existing owner/mentor stages again. Protected AP-001 sources must use their protected reviewer route; generic unit endorsement cannot mutate their source baseline.
- The generic old clubPlanState(APPROVED) shortcut is rejected for every demo persona, including Finance and Administrator; those labels cannot stand in for the approved institutional Finance Administrator and Principal assignments. Zero-cost plans get no exception.
- New `server/club-plan-governance-guard.mjs` runs after every generic engine command. It independently rejects manufacture of a raw approved/in-progress/completed club-plan version, changes/deletion of an existing approved material baseline, fabricated/rewritten unit review history and new/relinked club event records. The guard applies to unregistered legacy sources as well as AP-001-controlled sources; it does not rely on hiding a button or registering a plan first.
- Existing raw approved statuses/by/at/snapshots remain unchanged; no historical Finance/Principal decisions are fabricated. Existing activity links and learning/task/evaluation/report history remain usable. Derived approved-plan progress may still move between APPROVED/IN_PROGRESS/COMPLETED without altering the approved material baseline. New events cannot be created from a retained legacy approval or a unit endorsement; UI shows protected execution authorization pending. Opening an already linked event still works without creating another one.
- Updated card/overview copy explains mentor→Student Affairs unit review→Finance Administrator→Principal and separates final plan review from execution. Protected plan-review links remain. Added one form command/strict whitelist; existing service authentication, D1 schema and SQL save protocol are unchanged. Generic service CAS+audit commits unit endorsement and history atomically.

## Institutional and operational boundary

This owner-demo workspace still has no approved live institutional staff provider registry. Unit records are explicitly labelled OWNER_DEMO_UNIT_REVIEW and cannot satisfy institutional AP-001 decisions. The existing protected approval service remains default-disabled pending approved source/person/reviewer/fence configuration. Separate execution authorization and approved amendment propagation remain incomplete. New club-plan execution is therefore deliberately unavailable through generic demo commands, including from old APPROVED rows. Closing the shortcut is not completion of an institutional approval/execution rollout.

No real role, signing authority, payment, account or evaluation power was created. Existing student learning/evaluation authority is unchanged. Formation/committee authority, membership, budget settlement and annual learning review are distinct; their remaining institutional gaps are not marked complete. The wider Student Affairs placement/career pipeline and departmental monitoring cycle are recorded priorities, not claimed implemented by this guard.
